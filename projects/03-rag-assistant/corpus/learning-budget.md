# Learning and Development Budget

Owner: People Operations.

## Annual budget

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year. It can be used for courses, books, certifications, and conference tickets.

## Approval

Spending from the learning budget must be approved by your manager before purchase. Conference travel is paid from the travel budget, not the learning budget.

## Unused budget

Unused learning budget does not carry over into the next year.
