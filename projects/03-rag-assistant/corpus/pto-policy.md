# Time Off Policy

Owner: People Operations.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, plus local public holidays. Part-time employees receive PTO pro rata.

Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees have 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

All new parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first 12 months after a birth or adoption.
