# Remote and Hybrid Work

Owner: People Operations.

## Working model

Larkspur is a hybrid company. Teams agree their own office days, but everyone should be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone.

## Home office stipend

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair, or other ergonomic equipment. Claim it through Ledgerly within your first 90 days.

## Working from another country

You may work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 30 days in a calendar year. Each stay abroad must be approved in advance by the People Operations team, because of tax and immigration rules. Stays longer than 30 days are not permitted.

## Connectivity

When working outside an office, use the company VPN for access to internal systems and never use public Wi-Fi without it.
