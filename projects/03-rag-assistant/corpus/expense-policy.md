# Expense Policy

Applies to all employees of Larkspur Analytics (a fictional company used as sample data). Owner: Finance.

## What can be expensed

Reasonable costs incurred while doing company work can be expensed, including client meals, work travel, and approved software purchases. Personal entertainment, fines, and alcohol without a client present cannot be expensed.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance while travelling is $75 per person. Client meals are capped at $120 per attendee, including tax and tip.

Any single expense above $1,000 requires pre-approval from the Finance Director before the purchase is made. Expenses up to $1,000 are approved by your direct manager.

## Receipts and submission

Itemised receipts are required for every expense over $25. Submit expenses in Ledgerly within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.
