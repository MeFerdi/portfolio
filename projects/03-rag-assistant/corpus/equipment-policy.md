# Equipment Policy

Owner: IT.

## Laptops

Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if IT confirms a hardware fault.

## Peripherals

Employees may order one external monitor, a keyboard, and a mouse from the IT catalogue. Other peripherals are paid for from the home office stipend.

## Leaving the company

All company equipment must be returned within 10 business days of your last working day. IT sends a prepaid return box.
