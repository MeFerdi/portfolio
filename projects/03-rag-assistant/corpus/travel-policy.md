# Travel Policy

Owner: Finance and People Operations.

## Booking

All flights, hotels, and rail must be booked through TripDesk, the company travel portal. Bookings made outside TripDesk are only reimbursed if TripDesk had no availability, and you must attach a screenshot showing that.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a VP.

## Hotels

The standard hotel cap is $250 per night. In London, New York, San Francisco, and Zurich the cap is $350 per night.

## Per diem

Meals while travelling follow the daily meal allowance in the Expense Policy. Travel expenses are submitted the same way as other expenses.

## Safety

Register every international trip in TripDesk at least 7 days before departure so the security team can contact you in an emergency.
