# Incident Response

Owner: Platform Engineering.

## Severity levels

SEV1 means a full outage or confirmed data breach affecting customers. SEV2 means a major feature is degraded for many customers. SEV3 means a minor issue with a workaround.

## Paging

For a SEV1, the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes and open an incident channel. SEV2 pages must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. SEV3 issues are handled during business hours.

## Roles

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident has an incident commander who coordinates the response and a communications lead who updates the status page every 30 minutes.

## Postmortems

A written postmortem is required for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident within 5 business days. Postmortems are blameless: they focus on systems and processes, not individuals.
