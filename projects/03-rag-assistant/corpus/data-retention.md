# Data Retention Policy

Owner: Legal and Security.

## Customer data

Customer data is retained for 24 months after a contract ends and is then permanently deleted.

## Logs and backups

Application logs are kept for 90 days. Database backups are kept for 35 days.

## Deletion requests

Customer deletion requests must be completed within 30 days of receipt. Legal must be consulted before deleting data that is subject to a legal hold.

## Employee records

Employee records are retained for 7 years after employment ends, as required by tax law.
