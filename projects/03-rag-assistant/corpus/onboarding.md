# New Hire Onboarding

Owner: People Operations.

## Before day one

Your laptop is shipped to arrive at least 5 business days before your start date. You will receive login instructions by email on your first morning.

## First week

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy meets you on day one and checks in at least twice a week for your first month.

Complete the mandatory security awareness training during your first week.

## 30-60-90 plan

Within your first two weeks, agree a 30-60-90 day plan with your manager. Your manager reviews progress against it at the 30, 60, and 90 day marks.
